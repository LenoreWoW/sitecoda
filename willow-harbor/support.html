A little help from Willow Harbor

We’ll get you moving.

How do I play?

Start at the Harbor Post or visit the Town Square to enter Harbor Post, Corner Café, or Clocktower Vault. Each stop has its own tutorial, progress, hints and undo. All three stops are bundled for offline play.

Where is my progress?

Progress is saved separately on this device. It does not currently sync between devices. Avoid uninstalling or resetting unless you intend to erase progress.

I’m stuck

Hints and undo are always free. Restarting never removes earned stars, stamps, companion bonds, Guild ranks, or neighborhood restorations.

When does the daily challenge change?

Each mode uses a deterministic daily challenge that changes at midnight UTC.

Contact support

Email lenore.vg@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, mode, and what happened. Settings → Share diagnostics lets you optionally attach a local game log.
