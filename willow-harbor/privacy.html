Willow Harbor

Last updated October 19, 2026

A quiet coastal harbor.

This policy explains how Willow Harbor handles information, as published by Hassan Al-Sahli.

Progress on your device

All modes store progress, companion bonds, Guild ranks, settings and unfinished sessions locally. The game has no account, game-data server, or cross-device sync.

No advertising or tracking

Willow Harbor has no advertisements or third-party tracking. Optional Supporter Editions are processed natively by Apple StoreKit 2. We do not sell personal data.

Diagnostics you choose to share

A rolling log of recent game events and errors stays on your device. Nothing is sent automatically. “Share diagnostics” opens your device’s share sheet; you choose whether and where to send it.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachment you choose to send are used to respond and investigate your issue.

Deletion and retention

Settings → Reset all progress removes local game progress and diagnostics. Uninstalling removes app-local data. The App Store retains your purchase record under Apple’s policies.

Children

The game has no chat, social profiles, or user uploads. It is not submitted to the Kids category. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Your choices and contact

You can play offline, turn off harbor music and haptics, reduce motion, and clear local progress from Settings. For privacy questions, contact lenore.vg@gmail.com.
