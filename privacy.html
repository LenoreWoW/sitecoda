PRIVACY POLICY · 1 OCTOBER 2026

Your notebook stays with you.

SiteCoda is provided by Hassan Al-Sahli. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app, as well as this support website.

Your work records

Job details, customer information, notes, photos, signatures, checklists and issued reports are stored on your device. The app does not upload these records to a developer server. SiteCoda has no account, advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK.

Camera and selected photos

The app requests camera permission only when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker gives the app access to images you select. Imported images are resized and re-encoded; original image metadata is not copied to the saved report photo. The date shown for a photo records when you added it, not a verified capture time.

Purchases

Apple processes subscription payments. The app reads verified subscription entitlements using StoreKit to enable Plus. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may provide aggregate sales and performance information through its developer services.

Exports, backups and sharing

PDFs and notebook backups contain the information you entered, including customer details, photos and any acknowledgment. You choose where to save or share them. The destination service’s own privacy policy applies. SiteCoda does not automatically sync devices. Depending on your settings, Apple’s device backup services may include app data.

Retention and deletion

Records remain locally until you remove them. Issued reports preserve their original content even when you edit or remove a job. Delete a report separately in Records. A previous local save may be retained for recovery. Studio → Erase all records deletes your work, reports, photos, business details and checklists from the app. A local monthly job-creation counter remains to maintain the free allowance. Deleting app records does not delete copies you shared or exported, and does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Support and this website

If you email support, the information you choose to send is processed to respond to your request. Avoid sending customer photos or backup files unless they are needed and you intend to share them. This informational website uses no advertising or analytics scripts and no signup form. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the website.

Contact and changes

Contact hassan.a.alsahli@hotmail.com about privacy or support correspondence. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
