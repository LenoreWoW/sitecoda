HELP & SUPPORT

Keep the work moving.

SiteCoda helps independent maintenance contractors document work and share a clear customer handover. Use the steps below, or email us if something is not working.

Contact support ↗

Your first report

Create a job and choose a starting checklist or a blank job. Add or edit work items. Attach before, after or detail photos and explain what they show. Mark each item Done, Open or Return visit.

Choose Prepare handover and review the PDF. If the customer is present, they can optionally acknowledge receipt on your device. Confirm that you reviewed the report, issue it, then share the saved PDF.

Corrections and return visits

Editing a job does not change a PDF you already issued. Prepare a new handover to create the next version. For a separate visit, use Job actions → New visit at this property. This copies the property and client details into a new job.

Back up before changing devices

Open Studio → Export notebook backup and save the file to a safe place in Files. Import that file from Studio on the new device. Review the import summary before replacing the current notebook. Export the current notebook first if you need to keep both. Backups include photos and saved PDFs; the maximum file size is 200 MB.

Missing photos or recovery mode

Do not erase your notebook to troubleshoot. In Studio, try Restore previous save or import a complete backup. If a photo is still downloading from iCloud, open it in Photos first, then try attaching it again. Contact support with the message shown and the steps that led to it.

Free jobs and Plus

The free plan includes three new jobs per calendar month. Deleting a job does not reset that allowance. Plus adds unlimited new jobs, your own checklists, and report branding. You can always view, edit and export existing jobs and reports after Plus expires.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Use Restore purchases in the Plus screen while signed into the Apple Account used to subscribe. Manage or cancel through Apple’s subscription settings. Cancellation stops the next renewal; access normally continues until the current paid period ends. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What to include when asking for help

Send your device model, iOS version, SiteCoda version, the message you saw and what you were trying to do. Avoid sending customer photos, addresses, signatures or notebook backups unless you intend to share that information for support.
