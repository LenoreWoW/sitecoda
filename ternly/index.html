Your notebook belongs to you.

No account to create. No advertising or tracking SDKs. Tasks and focus history stay on your device. Export a backup before changing devices.

Ternly does not automatically sync notebooks between devices. Calendar access is optional, and Ternly never edits your events.

A few things to know.

Does Ternly block distracting apps?

No. Ternly is a planner and session timer. It records elapsed session time, which is not a measurement of attention.

What happens if the work does not fit?

Plus shows tasks that need more room before you apply a plan. They remain on your list without a scheduled time. Nothing is silently deleted or pushed into another day.

Can I cancel Plus and keep my tasks?

Yes. Cancel through your Apple Account subscriptions. Core tasks, focus, weekly history and backups remain available. Your notebook is not deleted when Plus ends.
