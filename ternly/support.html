A little guidance

We're here to help.

For a problem, a question, or an idea, email hassan.a.alsahli@hotmail.com.

Tell us your iOS version, Ternly version (in Settings), what you expected and what happened. Please omit private task details. Publisher: Hassan Al-Sahli.

Start your first day

Add a task, choose how long it needs, and give it a day. Tap its play button to focus, its title to edit, or its circle to mark it complete. Keep unscheduled ideas in Inbox. Routines add a task when you open a selected day.

Planning with Plus

Set your working hours and gaps in Settings. Optionally connect your calendar, then choose Make a plan. Review scheduled tasks and anything that does not fit before choosing Use this plan. All-day and free calendar events do not block the plan. Finish a running focus session before replanning.

A timer or notification seems wrong

The timer uses elapsed time and survives backgrounding or relaunching the app. Pause it when you take a break. Enable session-end alerts inside Ternly, and check notifications in iOS Settings. Focus modes, silent settings and notification previews can affect alerts. Ambient sound stops when you leave the app.

Restore Plus

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that purchased Plus. Restore recovers an active subscription; it does not restore your notebook. Purchases awaiting approval unlock after Apple confirms them. If the store is unavailable, free features remain available.

Cancel a subscription

Open Ternly Settings → Manage App Store subscriptions, or iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel before renewal to prevent the next charge. Access continues for the remaining paid period unless Apple indicates otherwise. Deleting the app does not cancel Plus. For purchase refunds, use Apple's purchase support.

Move to a new device

On the old device, choose Settings → Export backup. Save the JSON file somewhere you control. On the new device, choose Settings → Restore backup and select the file. Restoring replaces that device's notebook, so export any existing work first. A running timer is not resumed from an imported backup.

Delete your data

Choose Settings → Delete all local data. This removes the notebook on that device. Manage any exported files and device backups separately. No Ternly account exists to delete. A subscription must be cancelled separately through Apple.
