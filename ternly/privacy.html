Your day is yours.

This policy explains how Ternly, published by Hassan Al-Sahli, handles information in the iPhone and iPad app and on this website.

Your notebook

Tasks, notes, routines, daily reflections, preferences and focus history are stored locally on your device. Ternly does not operate an account service or upload your notebook to our servers. We do not include advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

Device backups managed by Apple may include app data according to your device settings. Exported notebook files contain your tasks and notes. Choose carefully where you save or share them. Ternly does not automatically sync notebooks between devices.

Calendar and notifications

If you enable the optional Plus calendar feature, Ternly requests calendar access through iOS. It reads timed busy events on your device to arrange tasks around meetings. It does not upload, edit or delete your calendar events. Calendar titles may appear on screen but are not stored in your notebook backup. You can disconnect the calendar inside Ternly or revoke permission in iOS Settings.

Session-end notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device. Ternly does not send push notifications from a server. Notification text may contain the focused task title, so use your iOS preview settings if you want to keep that hidden on your Lock Screen.

Purchases

Optional Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple. Ternly uses StoreKit to check verified purchase entitlements on your device. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple handles transaction information under its own privacy policy. Purchase and subscription records retained by Apple are managed through your Apple Account.

Support

If you email us, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request. Please avoid sending private task notes or backups unless needed and requested. You may ask us to delete support correspondence by email, subject to any retention obligations that apply.

This website

This website has no advertising, analytics scripts, user accounts or tracking cookies configured by us. The hosting provider processes technical request data, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and secure the website. External links lead to services with their own policies.

Your choices

Export your notebook, restore a backup, or delete all local data in Ternly Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device. Copies you exported or retained in device backups must be managed separately. Deleting data or the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, email hassan.a.alsahli@hotmail.com. Publisher: Hassan Al-Sahli. We will update the effective date on this page if the policy changes.
