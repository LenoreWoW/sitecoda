Before you start

Does my customer need the app?

No. You share a standard PDF they can open without SiteCoda.

Can I issue a report with unfinished work?

Yes. Open items, return visits and missing required after photos are listed in the summary. A report never automatically certifies that a property is safe or complete.

Where are my records stored?

On your device. Export a notebook backup in Studio to a safe location you choose. A backup contains customer information, photos and reports. SiteCoda does not provide automatic cloud sync.

Is the acknowledgment a verified digital signature?

No. It records a name and a signature drawn in person on your device, acknowledging receipt of the work record. It does not independently verify the signer’s identity.
