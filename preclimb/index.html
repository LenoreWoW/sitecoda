BUILT WITH LIMITS

The app knows what it cannot know.

Preclimb does not decide whether you are safe, diagnose symptoms, predict altitude response, replace your guide, or provide turn-by-turn mountain navigation.

Provider and guide instructions always override app templates. Turning around, changing the objective, or cancelling is recorded as a valid outcome—not a failure.
