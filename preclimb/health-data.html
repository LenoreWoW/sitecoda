CONSUMER HEALTH DATA PRIVACY POLICY

Health data never leaves your phone.

For residents of Washington (My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373) and Nevada (SB 370). Effective date: 22 October 2026. Regulated entity: Hassan Al Sahli, an individual developer, Doha, State of Qatar. Contact: hassan.a.alsahli@hotmail.com.

What consumer health data the app accesses

With your permission, Preclimb reads workout records from Apple Health on your iPhone: workout type, date, duration, distance and elevation. From those records the app derives broad training-history bands (for example consistency, longest effort, vertical exposure). Nothing else that could be called health data is accessed. The app also stores the fitness answers and training logs you type in yourself.

Source

Apple Health on your device, and you.

Why

To summarise your recent activity so the preparation plan starts from a realistic baseline. The bands are editable and you can use the app without granting Health access.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Health data is processed on-device only. It is never sent to us, never sent to any third party, and never stored in iCloud by the app. When you back up a trip to your own iCloud account, the app substitutes the bands you entered manually for any Health-derived band before the backup leaves the device.

Sharing and sale

We do not share or sell consumer health data. We do not use it for advertising. No third party receives it. There is no list of affiliates or processors because none exist for this data.

Your rights

  • See the data by opening Apple Health and the app's profile screen.
  • Withdraw consent by revoking the app's Health access in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Health.
  • Delete it by editing or resetting the profile in the app, or by erasing local app data.

Because we never receive the data, there is nothing for us to retrieve or delete on our side. If you still want to make a request or ask a question, email hassan.a.alsahli@hotmail.com. We respond within 30 days. If you disagree with our response, reply to the same address within 30 days and we will review it again; you may also contact the Washington Attorney General or the Nevada Attorney General.

Changes

We date every change to this policy and note material changes in the app.
