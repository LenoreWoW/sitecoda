SUPPORT

Product help, not emergency response.

For purchase, iCloud backup, data export, or product questions, email support@preclimb.com. Security concerns go to security@preclimb.com. Privacy and UK/EU data-subject inquiries go to privacy@preclimb.com.

Preclimb has no user accounts, so there is no password to reset and no account to recover. Your data is on your iPhone and, if you enabled backup, in your own iCloud account. Subscription refunds, billing, and the UK/EEA statutory 14-day cooling-off right of withdrawal are handled directly by Apple through your Apple ID and reportaproblem.apple.com.

Preclimb support is not an emergency, medical, rescue, route-safety, or on-mountain advice service. For an urgent situation, follow your operator's communication plan and contact the appropriate local emergency service.

When reporting a problem, do not email passports, policy numbers, medical records, diagnoses, or other sensitive documents. We keep support email only until your request is resolved and for up to 12 months afterwards.

Publisher & Trader Contact (EU DSA Articles 30–31): Hassan Al Sahli, Doha, State of Qatar (legal@preclimb.com).
