4. Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional and requested in context. The app asks only to read workouts so it can summarise your recent relevant activity. You can deny or revoke access in iOS Settings at any time and continue with manual answers and logging.

Health data is used on-device only. Raw HealthKit samples, imported workout records and the training-history bands derived from them are never uploaded anywhere and are never stored in iCloud by the app. When an iCloud backup is prepared, the app replaces any Health-derived band with the band you entered manually and strips device-origin workout identifiers and measurements from matched sessions. Your complete local history stays on the device.

We do not use Health information for advertising, marketing, sale, insurance or employment decisions, or rankings. We do not infer medical diagnoses or altitude tolerance. Physical fitness and training do not change your risk of altitude illness.

Residents of Washington and Nevada: see our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

5. iCloud backup

iCloud backup is optional and available to Preclimb Pro subscribers. When it is on, the app writes one backup file per trip to the private CloudKit database of the iCloud account signed in on your device. That data belongs to your iCloud account, counts against your iCloud storage, is encrypted by Apple, and is governed by Apple's iCloud terms and Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it and no ability to read, recover, or delete it on your behalf.

You can delete the backup at any time with Delete cloud backup in the app, or by managing app data in iCloud settings. If your device is not signed in to iCloud, or iCloud is restricted or full, backup is unavailable and the app keeps working locally.

6. Purchases

We receive no name, email, or payment information; we see subscription and sales reports in aggregate. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; manage it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data, or to complain to a regulator. For anything we hold (support email), write to privacy@preclimb.com and we will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm you sent the original message.
